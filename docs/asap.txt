The files "asap.jar" and "asap-8.0.0-java-src.zip" were provided by Fox.
The source is not included in the build.properties to keep the size at the minimum.

The "asap-code" points to the current development version of ASAP for testing changes.
The "asap.jat" and the folders "java/src" and "java/classes" are create my executing "make java/asap.jar" in a MSYS2 shell.
